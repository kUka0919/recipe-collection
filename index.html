1.Creamy Chicken Pasta

Ingredients

  • 300g dried pasta
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1.5 tbsp tomato puree
  • 50ml chicken stock
  • 150ml double cream
  • Salt&pepper to taste
  • Mozarella cheese
  • Parsley or coriander to finish

Steps

  1. Cut chicken into bite sized pieces and season with paprika, garlic powder, oregano, salt and pepper. Set aside.
  2. Chop the red onion and peppers
  3. Boil pasta for about 13-15 minutes, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
  4. Pan-fry the seasoned chicken 5-6 minutes until golden and cooked through. remove and set aside.
  5. In the same pan, cook onion and peppers 3-4mins. Stir in tomato puree then deglaze with chicken stock.
  6. Lower the heat, stir in the double cream, then return the chicken and pasta to the pan and mix well.
  7. Stir in grated mozarella until melted. Simmer gently 1-2 minutes.
  8. Remove from heat and finish with parsely or coriander

2. Hot Honey Wings

Ingredients

  • Chicken wings
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp BBQ sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder or flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Parsley and sesame seeds for garnish
  • Cooking spray

Steps

  1. Cut wings into two pieces and trim off the tip.
  2. Season with the spices and baking powder, mixing until all wings are evenly coated.
  3. Place on a rack over an oven dish and spray with cooking spray.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden brown (or cook in an air fryer).
  5. Combine honey, sriracha, soy sauce, BBQ sauce, rice vinegar, and chilli flakes in a pot and heat until bubbling.
  6. Toss the cooked wings in the sauce until fully coated.
  7. Garnish with parsley and sesame seeds.

3. Beef Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • 250g rump, fillet, topside, or flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 small red pepper, sliced
  • 1 small green pepper, sliced
  • 1 tsp garlic & ginger (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Kikkoman soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Stir fry sauce (any brand)

Steps

  1. Slice the steak thinly against the grain and season with black pepper and garlic powder.
  2. Heat a little oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Add the steak and stir-fry 2-3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside
  3. In the same pan, add the onion and peppers. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes until slightly softened but still crisp.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger (if using) and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Return the steak to the pan, then add the soy sauce and stir fry sauce. Mix untill well coated.
  6. Taste and add salt if needed.
  7. Serve hot with noodles or rice.

4. Potato Salad

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • 200g sausage, fried and chopped into bits
  • 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1.5 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 green pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Peel and cube the potatoes, then boil in salted water until fork-tender, about 15-20 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, hard-boil the eggs (about 10-12 minutes), then cool, peel, and chop.
  3. Fry the sausage bits until browned and cooked through.
  4. Drain the potatoes and let cool slightly.
  5. In a large bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, mustard, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Add the potatoes, chopped eggs, sausage, red onion, spring onion, and green pepper to the bowl.
  7. Gently fold everything together until evenly coated, being careful not to mash the potatoes.
  8. Chill in the fridge for at least an hour before serving.

5. Chocolate Chip Banana Bread

...and one sweet bonus recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 overripe bananas, mashed
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Dash of salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips, plus more for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease or line a loaf pan with parchment paper.
  2. Mash the bananas until smooth, then mix in the melted butter and Greek yogurt.
  3. Stir in the egg and vanilla extract, then add the brown sugar and granulated sugar and mix until smooth.
  4. Gently fold in the flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined- don't overmix.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips, then pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and sprinkle extra chocolate chips on top.
  6. Bake 35-40 minutes, until a toothpick comes out mostly clean.
  7. Cool in the pan 10-15 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
