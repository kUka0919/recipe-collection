Quick Easy Meals

Here's a collection of 5 recipes to some of my favorite easy to make meals

1.Creamy Chicken Pasta

Ingredients

  • 300g dried pasta
  • 2 large chcken breasts
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1.5 tbsp tomato puree
  • 50ml chicken stock
  • 150ml double cream
  • Salt&pepper to taste
  • Mozarella cheese
  • Parsley or coriander to finish

Steps

  1. Cut chicken into bite sized pieces and season with paprika, garlic powder, oregano, salt and pepper. Set aside.
  2. Chop the red onion and peppers
  3. Boil pasta for about 13-15 minutes, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
  4. Pan-fry the seasoned chicken 5-6 minutes until golden and cooked through. remove and set aside.
  5. In the same pan, cook onion and peppers 3-4mins. Stir in tomato puree then deglaze with chicken stock.
  6. Lower the heat, stir in the double cream, then return the chicken and pasta to the pan and mix well.
  7. Stir in grated mozarella until melted. Simmer gently 1-2 minutes.
  8. Remove from heat and finish with parsely or coriander

2. Hot Honey Wings

Ingredients

  • Chicken wings
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp BBQ sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp garlic powder or flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Parsley and sesame seeds for garnish
  • Cooking spray

Steps

  1. Cut wings into two pieces and trim off the tip.
  2. Season with the spices and baking powder, mixing until all wings are evenly coated.
  3. Place on a rack over an oven dish and spray with cooking spray.
  4. Bake at 200°C until golden brown (or cook in an air fryer).
  5. Combine honey, sriracha, soy sauce, BBQ sauce, rice vinegar, and chilli flakes in a pot and heat until bubbling.
  6. Toss the cooked wings in the sauce until fully coated.
  7. Garnish with parsley and sesame seeds.
